THE SCENT QUESTIONNAIRE

Find your signature.

Answer six questions to find a fragrance to explore. Choose “No preference” anywhere you’re open to suggestions.

1. Which fragrance family usually draws you in?
2. When do you expect to wear it?
3. What would you like to smell first?
4. Which note would you enjoy as the scent settles?
5. What kind of finish appeals to you?
6. Which part of the fragrance matters most to you?
