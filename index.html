THE HOUSE ARCHIVE

From garage to apothecary

  1. 1927 · The inspiration

    The old apothecary, with its dark wood, amber glass, and handwritten labels, set the mood for the Robofrags workshop. The scent direction draws on equally familiar materials: dry woods, green herbs, and warm spice.

  2. 2005 · A reference point for men’s fragrance

    The men’s fragrances of the early 2000s helped shape what the founder wanted to bring back: bright openings that settle into herbs, spice, and woods. Over time, the founder noticed that some familiar scents smelled different from the versions they remembered.

  3. 2017–2021 · Why some formulas changed

    In 2017, the European Union banned three fragrance allergens in cosmetics: HICC, atranol, and chloroatranol. Products containing them could no longer enter the EU market from August 2019 or be made available there from August 2021. The regulation acknowledged that reformulation could change how a fragrance smells. Read the EU regulation.

  4. October 2026 · The garage becomes an apothecary

    Robofrags began in a garage remodeled into an apothecary-inspired fragrance workshop. The founder wanted a line that brought back the character of classic men’s fragrance. Robofrags uses modern formulas and leaves out prohibited ingredients. The inspiration is the scent profile: crisp citrus, aromatic herbs, warm spice, and a dry wood finish.
