Fragrance bans, restrictions and reformulation

In 2017 the European Union adopted a ban on HICC, atranol, and chloroatranol in cosmetics following concerns about contact allergies. The change took effect in two stages. New products containing them could no longer enter the EU market from August 2019, and existing products could no longer be sold there from August 2021. Read the EU regulation.
